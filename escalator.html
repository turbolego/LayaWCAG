Laya WCAG 2.2 · continuous learning from an LLM

The decision model runs here in your browser (ONNX Runtime Web, WASM). When it does not know an answer — it abstains (low confidence) or the criterion is unbound (outside its trained distribution) — the uncertain cases are escalated to a real LLM teacher — a real Qwen on the free AI Horde worker network, a Qwen running in your browser via Transformers.js, or your own OpenAI-compatible endpoint. The teacher's verdict is learned in-browser, and the same cases stop needing the LLM on the next pass — exactly the laya.Escalator loop, with the learner simplified to a per-criterion delta head that fits in a browser tab. ← main WCAG demo
