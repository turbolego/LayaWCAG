The model sees markup text only — no CSS, no rendered layout, no assistive-technology simulation. Checks that need computed styles (contrast, reflow, target size) or behaviour (keyboard traps, focus order) are inherently out of reach here. The classifier was fine-tuned on a small dataset and its recall is weak: treat its output as a hint, never as a conformance verdict. Pair it with axe-core for rule-based checks.
